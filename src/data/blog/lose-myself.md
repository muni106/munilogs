## Context
Hi everyone.

In March 2027 I'll finish my master's degree in computer science engineering and I'm scared of losing the ability to learn how to code.

## But why?
Over the past year I've been trying coding agents to keep up with technology, understand how much is hype and how much is not, and I have to say they are revolutionary. They let you do in weeks what would have taken a year.

That's not the problem, though.
While I had control over stuff when I was moving slowly, I don't when I'm moving fast, and I feel like I'm cheating somehow.
That's not a big deal if I couple it with university where to effectively pass the exams I should know what I've done, making me open the editor and trying to understand what my code is doing.

On the other hand, if I start an SWE role, I'm not really sure I will be given the opportunity to read every line I generate, maybe the required pace will be too fast for me to slow down and actually learn while doing, or maybe we are moving to a new phase of software where you're no longer required to know what is written but only what the software does and how it's structured.

Everything is moving extremely fast, but I want to keep thinking with my own brain even if I'm wrong sometimes, it's a risk worth compared to losing ownership of my craft.
